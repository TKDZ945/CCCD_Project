Meet Our Docs

The people behind the care

Tiffany H. Ham, D.D.S., M.S.

BOARD CERTIFIED PEDIATRIC DENTIST

Dr. Tiffany is excited to be teaming up with Dr. Alexander Ham to help make your child's dental visits happy and magical. Her bubbly personality helps children feel at ease when they come in for their dental appointments. Dr. Tiffany grew up in Concord and is a Northgate High School Alumni (Go Broncos!). She received her B.A. in Molecular and Cell Biology from the University of California, Berkeley. At the University of California, San Francisco (UCSF) School of Dentistry, she earned her D.D.S. and was elected to the National Dental Honor Society of Omicron Kappa Upsilon (OKU). She continued her studies at UCSF and completed a three-year combined Master's-Pediatric dental residency with a M.S. in Oral and Craniofacial Sciences. Dr. Tiffany is a Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry and was recognized as a Pugh Award recipient for performing among the top 3% of Pediatric Dentists nationally.

Dr. Tiffany has served as a Board Director for the California Society of Pediatric Dentistry. She is a proud member of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry, American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry, California Society of Pediatric Dentistry, American Dental Association, California Dental Association, and Contra Costa Dental Society.

She has volunteered with various Bay Area community dental clinics and served on a dental mission trip to China with Alliance for Smiles. She is fluent in Mandarin.

Alexander Ham, D.D.S.

GENERAL DENTIST

Dr. Alexander Ham was born and raised in Oakland. He received his B.S. degree in Genetics at the University of California, Davis. He then attended the University of California, San Francisco (UCSF) School of Dentistry and also became a commissioned officer in the US Air Force. After graduating from UCSF, Dr. Ham completed an Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency at Sheppard AFB, Texas where he was the Chief Resident. Dr. Ham served as a US Air Force dentist for 4 years, having held the rank of Captain, and was the Chief of Clinical Dentistry at Edwards AFB, California. He currently serves as an Air Force Reservist.

Dr. Ham is a proud member of the American Dental Association, California Dental Association, and the Contra Costa Dental Society.

He and his wife, Dr. Tiffany H. Ham, live in Concord with their daughter, Zoey, and enjoy traveling, snowboarding, and spending time with family and friends. He also speaks Cantonese.
